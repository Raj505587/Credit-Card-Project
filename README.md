Credit Card Financial Dashboard 💳📊

Overview

Hey there! 👋 This project is all about turning complex credit card data into clear, actionable insights. I built an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps track everything from weekly revenue to customer spending habits - making it super easy for stakeholders to understand what's really going on with credit card operations.

What's This Project About?

Imagine having thousands of credit card transactions and customer records, and needing to make sense of it all. That's exactly what this dashboard does! It takes raw data from SQL and transforms it into visual stories that answer questions like:

Which cards are performing best?
Where's our revenue coming from?
What do our customers actually spend money on?
How are we doing week over week?

The Numbers That Matter

Here's what we're working with:

💰 $55M in total revenue
🔄 656K transactions processed
💵 $45M in transaction amounts
📈 $7.8M earned in interest
⭐ 646 customer satisfaction score

What I Found (The Interesting Stuff!)

The Blue Card Story :-

Blue cards are absolutely crushing it - they generate $46.1M (that's 83% of all revenue!). Meanwhile, premium cards like Gold and Platinum are underperforming, which actually presents a great opportunity for upselling.

Geography Matters :-

Texas, New York, and California are our heavy hitters, bringing in about $7.6M combined. There's definitely something working well in these states that we could replicate elsewhere.

Customer Insights :-

- High-income customers are our bread and butter ($7.5M).
- There's a notable gender gap - male customers contribute about 60% more revenue.
- Businessmen lead the pack with $34M in revenue.
- Interestingly, non-car owners generate more income than car owners.

Spending Habits :

People mainly use their cards for :-

1. Bills - $14M (the clear winner)
2. Entertainment - $10M
3. Fuel - $9M
4. Groceries - $9M
   
How I Built This

Step 1: Getting Data Ready
- Set up a SQL database (PostgreSQL)
- Imported CSV files with transaction and customer data
- Made sure everything was clean and ready to analyze
  
Step 2: Creating Smart Calculations

Used DAX to create useful metrics like:
- Age groups (20-30, 30-40, etc.)
- Income brackets (Low, Medium, High)
- Week-over-week revenue changes
- Total revenue calculations

Step 3: Building the Dashboards

Created two main dashboards:

- Customer Report - Who our customers are and what they're worth
  
- Transaction Report - What's happening with transactions and revenue

Conclusion

This dashboard isn't just about pretty charts - it's about understanding the story behind $55M in credit card operations. Every visualization answers a real business question, and every insight can lead to better decisions.
